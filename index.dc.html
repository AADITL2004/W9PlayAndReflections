04 · why emotions matter

why feelings matter ♡

"Along with effectiveness and efficiency, a designer must be aware of their users' responses, which are naturally emotional."

  1. 1

    Connection

    emotional design creates a deeper bond, and hence loyalty.

  2. 2

    Usability & Satisfaction

    usability and satisfaction are perceived as easier to use, contributing to positive emotions.

  3. 3

    Memorability

    a positive emotional response means users will recall the experience.

  4. 4

    Differentiation

    emotional designs stand out in a crowded market.

  5. 5

    Motivation & Engagement

    emotional designs can encourage greater engagement and motivate users more.

  6. 6

    User Well-being

    a good, calming design can contribute to a user's well-being.

  7. 7

    Brand Perception

    emotions associated with a product reflect on the brand as a whole.

    Engage users as a character

    engage users as part of the experience rather than treating them as passive visitors.

  3. 3

    Colour and contrast

    use colour and contrast advantageously.

  4. 4

    Appropriate terms and phrases

    craft appropriate terms and phrases.

  5. 5

    Consistency across micro-labels

    maintain consistency across micro-labels.

    Sound and video
